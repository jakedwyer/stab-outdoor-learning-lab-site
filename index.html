ABOUT THIS LANDSCAPE

A vision of what’s possible.

The design views preserve the supplied conceptual imagery. The interactive walkthrough follows the campaign’s Learning by Doing case for support. It is a spatial interpretation, not a school-approved construction design. Final features, dimensions, planting, and grades will evolve.

The retaining wall between the football field and play space is included. It is shown at approximately three feet, with the football field on the raised side, as confirmed by the project brief. Detailed dimensions remain conceptual.
